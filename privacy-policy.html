Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026.

This policy covers the bbtestb application operated by BB Test Biz.

What the app accesses

bbtestb connects to the Pinterest API using our own business account only. It reads our own Pins, boards, and account information, and creates or schedules Pins on our own boards at our direction.

What we store

We store only the access tokens needed for the integration and basic internal reports about our own content. We do not access, store, or share data belonging to other Pinterest users.

Sharing

We do not sell or share any data with third parties.

Deletion and contact

Access tokens can be revoked at any time from our Pinterest developer dashboard. Questions: edhopalu+pinterest-b@gmail.com
