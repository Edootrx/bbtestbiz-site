BB Test Biz

BB Test Biz is a small digital content studio based in Indonesia. We create and schedule visual content for our own brand channels and prepare simple performance reports for our team.

Our app: bbtestb

bbtestb is our private, single-user integration with the Pinterest API. It is used only by our own team, only on our own Pinterest business account, to:

  • Create and schedule Pins for our own boards.
  • Read our own Pins, boards, and account data to prepare internal reports.

The app does not serve third parties, does not collect data from other Pinterest users, and does not display ads. Access is limited to our own account data (pins, boards, and user account information for our business account).
